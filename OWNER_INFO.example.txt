Read100 Observer / Owner Runtime Configuration Example
======================================================

Local/remote URLs and credentials are environment-specific.
Do not commit real Owner PIN or Observer PIN values.

Recommended environment variables:
- READ100_OWNER_PIN=<provision outside Git>
- READ100_OBSERVER_PIN=<provision outside Git>
- READ100_REMOTE_PILOT=1 for remote pilot mode

Runtime config:
- Copy config.example.json to config.json locally.
- Replace placeholder participant codes with provisioned pilot codes outside Git.
